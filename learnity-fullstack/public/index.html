// 01. About Learnity

Built to Make Classroom Learning Active, Collaborative & Memorable.

Learnity (The Lively Teaching Project) was founded by educators, engineers, and senior university mentors with a single goal: to transform traditional one-way lessons into high-energy, workshop-style learning environments.

Instead of memorizing static slides, students in Learnity cohorts work in small teams of 10–12 peers. Every module combines foundational theory with live demonstrations, structured debates, and weekly hands-on builds—ensuring every participant leaves with real competence and a documented portfolio.
